Mini fuel stations for businesses
A range for internal fuel dispensing, mobile supply, northern climates and sites with different tank requirements.
Business Monoblock
The Business Monoblock container fuel station stores and dispenses petroleum products and is available with a single- or double-wall tank. It can be used for internal or commercial operations and installed both within and outside populated areas.
Fuel trailer
The fuel trailer stores and dispenses light petroleum products and can be transported when empty at speeds up to 35 km/h. The fuel dispenser is installed to the customer’s specifications.
Arctic
The Arctic container fuel station stores and dispenses petroleum products and has a double-wall tank. It can be used for internal or commercial operations and installed both within and outside populated areas.
North
The North container fuel station stores and dispenses petroleum products in temperatures as low as −50°C. It has a double-wall tank and can be used for internal or commercial operations, both within and outside populated areas.
Prestige
The Prestige mini fuel station stores and dispenses petroleum products and has a double-wall tank. It can be used for internal or commercial operations and installed both within and outside populated areas.
Fuel module
The fuel module stores and dispenses petroleum products and has a single-wall tank. It is intended solely for internal fuel dispensing and may be installed outside populated areas.
